  sa2360/pddl
    moonpddl is a MoonBit PDDL planning engine and standalone plan validator supporting PDDL 2.1 Level 1 with numeric fluents. It parses domain and problem files, grounds them into a state-space task, searches with heuristics such as hmax, hadd, and hff under GBFS, A*, or weighted A*, and independently replays plans to check applicability, goals, and cost.

Run `moon add <module>@<version>` to add a dependency.
